Song by Cigarettes After Sex_2017

I clicked on this song on a cloudy day.
The name of it pulls me back to the last summer in high school.
Anonymous questionnaire that began with: Most likely to_.

It was an old tradition.
I remembered flipping through the senior page in the yearbook when I was still an elementary kid.
All the big kids posing with props, and on the bar below them, a description of what they’re most likely to do.
It was always fun looking at those.
I always did for years, one of the few “must-dos” after getting my hands on the book in June.

I spend most of my school life living in my mind.

I didn’t expect anything.
So I was surprised I got my own descriptions.
I was thankful before reading anything.

I’m lost in running thoughts.

It was a cloudy day when I walked through Central Park again.
I love to start from the north, and walk through and out the south.

Sometimes I wonder if a seagull will fly across the ocean.

I was playing this song.

The gray sky is no longer cold.
It’s quiet.
Like I was left alone above layers of dirt and concrete.

This song brings me back to the afternoon when I got my own line
“Most likely to be calm during an apocalypse”

Funny to see myself in the words of others.
Funny that’s how I remembered this word and not by the book of revelation.
Funny how I kept wondering who wrote this.

The past memories always find me in the most unexpected ways.

Song by Pink Floyd_1971

A few seconds before the plane took off.
I’d put on my headphones.
Along with the rumble of the great vessel accelerating, I closed my eyes.

I can feel the carpet through the bottom of my shoe.
Underneath it must be all the metal boards and whatever was keeping this whole thing operating.

How many times would I have to play this song on repeat until landing?
I thought.

I wouldn’t hear it though.
Because I'd be sound asleep.

I couldn’t remember the first time I heard this song.
It might be because of my curiosity, I clicked into a song that's 23 and a half minutes long.
The album cover looked like a cave at first.

It brought me back to that instance, to the first beam of sunlight that shone through the cave mouth.
The large stalactites.
The cold underground stream.
The bats…did I actually see one?
The moisture in the air.
The moment when everyone was quiet.

Then I found out it was an ear submerged in ripples of water.

I remember waking up in the middle of the song, I don’t know how many times it played until I finally got to hear it with a half lucid mind.
Like stepping outside the cinema after a long movie.
But I was sitting in the plane. Seatbelt fastened. Dehydrated.

Music by Franz Schubert. Poem by Johann Wolfgang von Goethe. Animation by Jeremy Hamway-Bidgood, created by Oxford Lieder_2013

I have a weird, almost superstitious theorem.
If I just run through every worst-case scenario that could possibly happen in the world,
I might not panic as much when it actually hits me.

I was walking in a winter night,
There was an artificial lake between the school and the subway station.
But the prelude to this song began, what was in my mind was not about the cold crisp air nor the creaking wooden trestle.

It was many years ago, in that memory where I can't even remember the time and the place, left me with a faint memory of a music video with uncanny shadow puppet-like characters.
A strange sublime fascination emerged out of my indescribable unknown.

But I never knew what I was looking at.
Until many years later I found the same music video, the name of the song, and the whole story that answered my unknown.

What are the possibilities of feeling a withering life in your hands?

Clearly, I had not given this thought long enough for me to prepare.

And so, the day when my patch of blue left, one afternoon that window had been left ajar.
The crease that let the Erlkönig in, he lured away the breath under all those feathers.
I didn’t even hear a note of his enchantment.
It seems that I have fallen into a state that will recur many years later,
A state that lies somewhere between reality and nothing.

I’d rather have him fly away than remember how life slowly ebbed away in my hands.

Years later in that winter wind
For a long period of time,
Perhaps it was due to some kind of taboo mentality
I’d always skip the last line.

Song by Green Day_2009

I can never seem to live in the time and space I find myself in.

It feels as if my mind has been torn into pieces.

One part keeps wandering through the past.
Another keeps drifting endlessly into the future.
But my body remains confined to the present.

Perhaps time machines have already been invented.

I always find myself traveling to the future at certain moments in the present.

I would tell myself: I'll probably miss this moment before long.
And then I would try desperately to remember the tiniest details.
But time always seems to work against me, as if it deliberately speeds up whenever I do this.

One afternoon
I came home from class

I just stood there.
Unable to do anything.

How I wished I could be like the Little Prince,
Simply move my chair a little and continue watching the same scenery.

When the sea breeze brushed against my face,
The reddish haze left by the drink was blown away.

I'll be leaving soon.

The sea shimmered in the light.
And time accelerated once again.

So this is how it goes.

I worry about things that haven't happened.
I grieve for love that never even began.
