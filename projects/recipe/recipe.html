Before you start: Please prepare a piece of paper and a pen. (or take your notes on your computer) Read ALL instructions!!!

Recipe:

  1. Open Chrome.
  2. Search “Merriam-Webster Word of the Day.”
  3. Read the Word of the Day. As you read, jot down any random thoughts, words, or phrases that come to mind.
  4. Count how many words or phrases you wrote down.
  5. Open the same number of tabs in Chrome.
  6. Enter one of your words or phrases into each tab and search.
  7. Open the first link that comes up in each tab.
  8. Read the title and the first sentence of the paragraph section.
  9. Write down your first thought or word that comes to mind after reading it. If nothing comes up, you don’t have to write anything down.
  10. Repeat Steps 4–9 for every tab for another round.
  11. Continue the entire process (Step 1-10) only once. Do not go back for another round. Take a screenshot of your Chrome window with all the tabs open.

IMPORTANT: Do not close any tabs during the process. Keep all the tabs open until you finish. Do not go back during the process.

After the process:

  • Organize the branch of ideas you just created. Look at the connections between each branch, word, or phrase. Are there any connections? Can you link them together? If not, that’s okay!
  • Please upload your screenshot and all your notes to me. Thanks for participating!
